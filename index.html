Your Money Needs a Strategy

Having money to invest is only the beginning.

As your income grows, your financial decisions become more important. The money you are investing today should be working towards something meaningful in the future.

That could mean creating the option to retire before 65, building long-term financial independence, or creating an investment portfolio capable of producing an income stream later in life.

The objective isn't simply to accumulate as much money as possible. It's to build wealth with a clear purpose behind it.

Start With the Right Questions

Before choosing investments, understand what you're trying to achieve.

Your current position, your goals, your time horizon and the amount you're able to invest all influence the strategy that makes sense.

1. What are you currently doing to build wealth for your future?

2. What would you ultimately like your wealth to achieve?

3. How much could you comfortably allocate towards building your wealth each month?

4. When would you ideally like to have the option to retire?

Retirement Is a Number, Not an Age

The goal isn't simply to retire. It's to build enough wealth to give yourself options.

Retirement planning isn't simply about reaching a certain age. It's about knowing the number you need to achieve for your investments to produce your desired income without eating into your capital.

That means thinking about what you want your future to look like, how much income you'll need, how long your investments have to grow, and how today's decisions affect tomorrow's options.

Build for the Future

Your investments should work towards more than just a balance on a statement.

Tax Efficiency

Tax should form part of the conversation — not drive the strategy.

If you're earning a higher income, tax efficiency should form part of the investment conversation.

Depending on your circumstances, certain retirement contributions and investment structures may provide tax advantages.

But tax savings shouldn't be the reason you build your wealth strategy. They're one part of a bigger picture that also includes your goals, future needs, investment timeframe, risk profile and desired lifestyle.
